FAQS FAQ

How do I join? Is it free?

Create your free account with us and become a member of our Key Club. You'll receive 5 keys (=$5) just for joining.

How do I earn keys?

For every $10 you spend on qualifying purchases, you receive 1 key. Keys post to your account after your order ships.

How do I view my key balance?

Sign in to your account and open your Key Club dashboard to see your current balance, tier, and recent activity.

How do I redeem keys?

At checkout, apply your available keys toward your order. Every 10 keys equals $1 off, applied before tax where eligible.

What does the key status mean?

Your status reflects your recent activity and unlocks the perks shown on this page, including birthday gifts and VIP access.

Do my keys expire?

Key Club keys may expire if your account is inactive for an extended period. See the loyalty terms for the current policy.

What are the Terms & Conditions?

The Key Club program terms describe eligibility, earning, redemption, and limitations. Read the full terms on our legal page.
